Production Line Counter

Unipak LLC · Manufacturing Engineering Intern
ScopeDesign, build, install
HardwareESP32 · 24V I/O
EnclosureSolidWorks · PLA+
StatusRunning daily

Problem

The plant had no live view of what each line was producing or how long it sat idle, so downtime went unmeasured.

What I built

  • The lines had no sensor to count from, so each counter is a standalone unit with its own photoeye, powered from the 24V DC supply in the line's PLC cabinet. The sensor signal comes in through optocoupler isolation.
  • Enclosure and adjustable mount designed in SolidWorks and printed in PLA+.
  • Each node serves its own live dashboard (count, rate, shift timer, downtime) over plant Wi-Fi. A central page shows every line at once, with per-shift Excel export.
  • Two line counters built and installed, plus a third node for a checkweigher that reads its accept / over / under signals. The checkweigher node is built and bench-tested but not yet wired to the machine.
Result ~2 hr / day

of downtime found on the main cartoner line, traced to breaks starting early and the line restarting late. The counters are still running.

Container Labeling Jigs

Unipak LLC · Manufacturing Engineering Intern
ScopeFixture design
CADSolidWorks
MaterialPETG, FDM
StatusCustomer approved

Problem

Some containers couldn't be labeled by the lines, so workers applied labels by hand. Placement varied from piece to piece, and a customer sent product back.

What I built

  • Fixtures that nest the container and locate the label, so every label lands in the same place.
  • Modeled around the actual container in SolidWorks, then printed in PETG for durability on the floor.
  • Instructions printed into the part in English and Spanish (“Label face down / Etiqueta hacia abajo”) for the operators who use it.
Result

The customer reviewed the jigs on-site and approved them for use. Unipak then asked for a second version for its smaller containers.

EK Center Console Parts

HamrTek · In development
FitsEK Civic, K-swap & stock
CADSolidWorks
ReferenceiPhone 3D scan
StatusModeling

Problem

Swapping a K-series engine into an EK Civic moves the shifter and its cables into the cupholder's space. Most owners cut the cupholder out or remove it, which leaves an open hole in the console.

What I'm building

  • A tray that fills the cupholder opening and routes and hides the shifter cables underneath it.
  • Scanned the factory center console myself with an iPhone 13 Pro Max, then designed against the scan so the tray matches the OEM surfaces and openings.
  • A redesigned cupholder for stock, non-swapped cars is also in progress. The factory cupholder holds drinks poorly and is a common complaint among owners; the redesign replaces it in the same space.

1996 Civic Hatchback Build

Personal project · Engine build, swap & wiring
Chassis1996 Honda Civic (EK)
EngineLS/VTEC, forged
WorkBuilt by me, start to finish
StatusRunning

Why it's here

Building this engine is what made me want to study mechanical engineering. My HamrTek parts grew out of working on this car.

What I did

  • Built an LS/VTEC engine: a B18B1 bottom end with a B16A VTEC head. I assembled both the bottom end and the top end myself, with forged internals, ARP head studs, and a Cometic head gasket.
  • Measured and verified every clearance and tolerance during assembly against spec.
  • Installed the engine and wired a coil-on-plug ignition conversion and a new ECU into the chassis harness. The engine is built and running.
  • TIG-welded a repair on a section of the exhaust, working alongside an experienced welder.
